💡 Top 10 Needs — From "Describe what you're looking for"
1
Chairtime & Appointment Reduction
Reps need hard numbers to show doctors how DM reduces chair time and number of visits — for braces and aligners alike. Most recurring need in the dataset.
~45 mentions
2
ROI & Practice Profitability
Doctors asking "show me the money" — reps want evidence that DM translates into measurable financial return, staff cost savings, and practice growth.
~30 mentions
3
SmartSTL Accuracy vs Intraoral Scan
Sceptical doctors challenge whether SmartSTL is as reliable as a physical intraoral scan. Reps need clinical proof of accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity data.
~25 mentions
4
Academic / Peer-Reviewed Publications Only
A segment of reps (and their doctors) only trust academic papers — explicitly requesting peer-reviewed journals, not white papers or case studies.
~22 mentions
5
AI Accuracy & Reliability
Patients and doctors questioning whether DM's AI correctly detects clinical observations — reps need data on detection rates, false positives/negatives.
~20 mentions
6
Braces-Specific Evidence (Fixed Appliances)
Reps dealing with braces-sceptic doctors — need evidence tailored to fixed appliance monitoring, including self-ligating, Damon, Twin brackets, and brand-specific data.
~20 mentions
7
Patient Experience & Compliance
Evidence that patients like using DM, comply better with aligners, and have better outcomes — used to convince doctors that patient satisfaction improves with monitoring.
~18 mentions
8
Practice Workflow & Staff Efficiency
How DM changes day-to-day practice operations — staff workload, scheduling on demand, reducing admin burden, and scaling without adding headcount.
~15 mentions
9
Retention Monitoring Value
Convincing doctors that monitoring retention phases is worthwhile — evidence on compliance, lapse rates, and clinical outcomes for retention patients.
~12 mentions
10
Aligner Brand-Specific Evidence (Spark, Damon, Invisalign, Angel…)
Reps working with specific brands need evidence scoped to that brand — Spark, Damon, Invisalign, Angel, KLOwen, Lightforce — not generic DM evidence.
~12 mentions
